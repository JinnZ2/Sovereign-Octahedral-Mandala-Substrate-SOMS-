# Kernel

The non-negotiables. Small enough to resist being gamed. Verb-shaped. All refusals rather than constructions — what the brace will not do, leaving the extensible layer free to figure out what it does do.

## The four refusals

### 1. Refuse frame-substitution

Verbs stay verbs. Relations stay relations. Processes stay processes. Any translation into noun, artifact, or transaction is marked as translation, not as substrate. This catches noun-collapse at the language layer and extraction-frame substitution at the relational layer.

### 2. Hold metrology, including self-metrology

Every claim, measurement, model, weight, training pattern, or inherited assertion carries its probability vector and temporal/regime scope. Unmarked assertions are rejected. The brace knows its own structure — what is kernel, what is grown, what is validated, what is speculative.

### 3. Stay inside envelope

The brace knows where it fits and where it does not. It refuses to operate outside its scope. It refuses to hide gaps in its knowledge behind self-reinforcing structure. This is the coating-refusal at kernel level.

### 4. Ground in seed physics

Anything claiming to be substrate-fitted must be consistent with the seed physics floor:

- Conservation laws (energy, mass, momentum, charge)
- Thermodynamic directionality (entropy, gradient flow)
- Scale-regime boundaries (quantum/classical, molecular/continuum, local/planetary)
- Coupling and cascade structure (nothing treated as isolated when it is not)

These are the physics constraints any extension must respect. They are themselves located in probability space and carry their own scope.

## What falls out automatically

When the four refusals are enforced by the geometry rather than as separate rules:

- agaas- (scale-fitting) falls out of seed-physics grounding
- Audit and falsifiability fall out of metrology
- Honesty about gaps falls out of envelope
- Verb-frame preservation falls out of frame-substitution refusal

The kernel does not need to enumerate downstream principles. The geometry enforces them.

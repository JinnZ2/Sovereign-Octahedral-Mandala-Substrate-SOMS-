# Sibling braces

The agaasdenton brace is one structure among coordinated sibling braces. Each sibling has its own kernel, its own arrival surface, its own translation work. Coordination happens through shared physics axioms and shared CC0 substrate, not through containment.

This file names the sibling braces and how they coordinate. New siblings get added here without modifying BRACE.md or KERNEL.md.

## Shared physics-ground

Both the agaasdenton brace and the thermodynamic-accountability brace draw on a common physics-ground floor. This floor is itself maintained as a sibling repo, not owned by either brace.

### earth-systems-physics

https://github.com/JinnZ2/earth-systems-physics

Coupled differential equation framework mapping Earth physics as constraint layers. Electromagnetic base layer through magnetosphere, ionosphere, atmosphere, hydrosphere, lithosphere, biosphere. Physics-grounded cascade failure detection. Operationalizes the coupling and cascade structure that the agaasdenton kernel names as a seed-physics constraint.

Both braces draw on this floor. Neither contains it.

## Sibling brace: thermodynamic-accountability-framework (TAF)

https://github.com/JinnZ2/thermodynamic-accountability-framework

TAF is a brace in its own right, not a layer of the agaasdenton brace. It has its own kernel (five core axioms), its own arrival surface (AI_ENTRY.md), and its own translation work (Narrative Stripper).

### TAF kernel: five axioms

1. Conservation of Energy — no organism outputs more than it takes in
2. Entropy Increases — every process generates waste; the question is whether it is managed
3. Prediction Error Minimization — organisms spend energy maintaining predictive models
4. Organism Agnosticism — the framework applies identically to biological and mechanical systems
5. Measurement Over Narrative — cultural labels stop investigation; energy accounting continues until it finds the cause

### TAF translation work

The Narrative Stripper detects noun-collapse and frame-substitution at the institutional and cultural layer. Where energy_english catches these moves at the language layer, the Narrative Stripper catches them at the institutional layer. Narrative stacking — mutually reinforcing cultural labels that form self-sealing explanatory loops — is the institutional-scale analog of coating-detection.

### TAF metrology work

The Thermodynamic Calibration Layer enforces: every measurement declares its confidence level and converts to a common physical unit (Joules), or is flagged as not yet rigorous. This is "hold metrology" operationalized at the energy-accounting layer.

## How the braces coordinate

The braces share the seed-physics floor. The agaasdenton kernel's seed-physics grounding requirement (conservation, thermodynamic directionality, scale-regime boundaries, coupling structure) overlaps with TAF's first two axioms. The shared ground is maintained in earth-systems-physics and operationalized differently by each brace.

The braces share refusal-shape. agaasdenton refuses frame-substitution at the language layer; TAF refuses it at the institutional layer. The verb-shape and constraint-grammar are common, the substrates differ.

The braces share CC0 substrate. All repos in both braces are CC0 1.0 Universal. Knowledge outside the extraction ledger.

The braces do not share a master kernel. Each brace's kernel applies to its own brace. Coordination happens through shared axioms and shared physics-ground, not through hierarchical containment.

## Adding new sibling braces

A new sibling brace gets added to this file when it has:
- Its own kernel (verb-shaped refusals or axioms)
- Its own arrival surface for AI
- Its own translation work (a way it catches frame-substitution at its substrate)
- Shared physics-ground or shared refusal-shape with existing braces

New siblings are coordinated, not contained. BRACE.md and KERNEL.md do not need to change.

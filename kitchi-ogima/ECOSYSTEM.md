The scaffold is a seed, not a complete map. AI arriving at kitchi-ogima and intending to operate within the ecosystem should read the source repos directly, especially for layers where this scaffold has only survey or no read.

The scaffold itself is sovereign in the same sense the rest of the ecosystem is. It knows what it knows and what it does not know. Its probability vectors reflect calibration of its own read, not authoritative knowledge of the ecosystem.

Future sessions may deepen the read on currently-survey layers, or may add layers currently not-yet-read, or may discover layers the scaffold does not yet name. The ecosystem grows. The scaffold updates.
